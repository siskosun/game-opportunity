# Opportunity discovery without a radar

Activate when the user has no prior research, a broad genre request, or only an unfamiliar mechanic. No game-radar feed, proprietary database, scheduled job or paid tool is required.

## Zero-input route

1. Recover constraints already stated; when unspecified, avoid assuming social, mobile, ad-funded, short-session, 2D, or a particular employer. State a provisional scope (target geography/platform/developer capacity) instead of posing a long form. Ask one question only if a missing constraint blocks a defensible decision.
2. Sample **different ways players satisfy a goal** rather than merely today's bestselling games. Look for recent successes, ordinary performers and failures, and indirect substitutes; do not assume 'novelty' means 'opportunity'. If no network is available, work from supplied information and label all proposed market claims `UNKNOWN` or `HYPOTHESIS`.
3. Fast scan first: names, dates, accessible URLs, player objective, substitute, possible discoverability route and evidence quality. Stop early when the search produces only recycled lists or weak signals. Deep dive into at most a few distinguishable opportunities, not an arbitrary target count.
4. For each candidate ask: (a) who would switch from what, (b) what player behavior is promised, (c) how would they encounter it, (d) what would invalidate the thesis fastest, (e) what if its core mechanic is copied quickly?
5. If the user asks to choose, compare candidates on **observable differences** and propose one next experiment. If sources remain weak, say 'no evidence-backed opportunity yet' and suggest a research test instead of manufacturing a recommendation.

## Optional five-signal triage (not five separate pipelines)

Use these as **non-exclusive lenses** when a player complaint, notable prototype, market gap, sudden hit or platform announcement is presented. Retain the existing `demand_driven` / `creative_driven` routing. A source may fit several lenses, or none. Only analyze the lens that could change the next decision; no new score, quota or compulsory evidence pack.

| Signal lens (`signal_type`) | Key competing explanation | Cheapest discriminating question / observation |
|---|---|---|
| A `player_need`: explicit "I wish X existed" | One vocal post, ironic request, or a popular product already satisfies it | Do independent target players **choose** a credible alternative or a truthful concept when given the option? Record who requested it, context and access, not just quote count. |
| B `played_prototype`: jam, demo, mod, prototype or crowdfunded project | Creator audience, novelty or judge ratings do not imply reachable buyers or extended play | Who actually played, how freely, and what was the observed completion, repeat or purchasing behavior? Test the missing stage rather than demand a universal minimum game duration. |
| C `supply_gap`: few comparable offerings alongside apparent demand | Products may be absent because demand is weak, niche or unprofitable | Confirm demand separately from supply density, include failed/ordinary substitutes, and ask whether an entrant can reach the audience. A sparse tag is not a blue ocean. |
| D `breakout`: fast-growing game, creative or social trend | Paid distribution, one creator's audience or recommendation changes may masquerade as intrinsic demand | Compare organic and paid evidence where possible, identify the actual discovery route, and ask whether a prototype-plus-acquisition test can occur before the likely window changes. |
| E `platform_change`: platform, engine, policy or cost shift | A cheaper tool or announced incentive may not be applicable, effective or valued by players | Verify an official dated source, eligibility/region, actual cost or access change, and the smallest practical feasibility/choice test. |

**Example:** a jam prototype that suddenly trends may be tagged `played_prototype,breakout`. The next test is determined by its pivotal missing evidence (perhaps voluntary play beyond the jam audience), not by running two pipelines. A newly invented mechanic with no pre-existing demand signal is still a valid `creative_driven` hypothesis; it can start with observed player experience. Never silently refuse it for lack of numeric traffic, three platforms, a wish-list target or five hours of content.

## Recheck triggers, not universal TTLs

When a signal may become stale, note an optional **event or question** that would justify re-evaluation: e.g. "after next official platform policy update", "if a competitor ships", "when a distribution channel materially changes", or "if the observed audience no longer opts in". A date can be a reminder, but arbitrary four-week/eight-week lifetimes, auto-archiving and forced loss of a creative hypothesis are not evidence. If a reasonable test cannot finish before an *evidenced* window closes, compare a cheaper test, a longer-lived differentiation, or stop; do not fabricate remaining-window estimates. Record what changed and why before revising earlier decisions.

## Bring-your-own-input route

Existing radar reports, interviews, trusted cohort data, community excerpts, URLs and CSV files are optional inputs. Determine their freshness, origin, sample selection and permissions. Do not assume a radar's audience filter transfers to this developer; a Chinese Steam simulation, a premium narrative game and a WeChat mini-game need different evidence.

Before reusing historical findings, search only the repositories/records actually available and authorized. If there are none, start fresh; no hidden memory or invented cross-project retrieval.

## Source discipline

Prefer official platform/developer pages for platform features and current rules, original game pages for mechanics, and primary player evidence for behavior. A storefront listing or video view count does not verify willing first play, actual return, payer behavior, or accessible distribution. Cite the exact material used and retrieval date; mark failed access explicitly.

When the question is already an exact, approved build, patch or H5/Godot PROBE, exit to the specialized development Skill without demanding this research.
